A tutor that adapts to who the learner is, not just what they got wrong.

PIA is a Personal Instructing Agent for Grade 7 mathematics. It profiles each student against the Big Five personality inventory, then matches a teaching persona to that profile — pacing, tone and hinting all shift to suit the learner in front of it.

One learner, one profile, one matched agent

Every participant is measured before they are taught, and the agent they meet is chosen by that measurement rather than by chance.

Personality-first profiling

Students complete the Big Five Inventory before any tutoring begins. Scoring runs server-side, so the profile that drives the session cannot be edited from the browser.

Personas that actually differ

Each agent persona changes how a problem is scaffolded — how early a hint arrives, how much encouragement carries it, how quickly difficulty climbs after a streak.

Built for clean data

Stage gates, device limits and consent tracking are enforced in the database, not the interface — so a session that shouldn't have happened simply cannot.

How a session runs

Four stages, opened one at a time by the research team so the whole cohort moves together.

1

Personality inventory

A 50-item Big Five questionnaire establishes the learner's trait profile.

2

Agent assignment

A persona is matched to the profile — or chosen freely, depending on the condition.

3

Guided practice

The agent works through Grade 7 problems, adapting hints and difficulty live.

4

Post-test

Results are compared against the pre-test baseline for every condition.
