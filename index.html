How a lesson works

Big problem?
Cut it into panels.

Percentage problems can be broken into smaller steps. A ₱100 bag now costs ₱120. What is the percentage increase? This example shows the calculation.

  1. The problem

    ₱100 →to ₱120

    PIA: The price went up. By what percent? Let’s find out.

  2. Find the change

    ₱120 − ₱100 ₱20 more

    PIA: First, how much did it go up? New price minus old price.

  3. Compare to the start

    20 ÷ 120 100 = 0.20

    PIA: Divide by the ORIGINAL price, where it started. That’s the whole trick.

  4. Done

    20%

    Check: 20% of ₱100 = ₱20 ✓

    Click!

Self-determination theory

Support for your learning

PIA’s design draws on autonomy, competence and relatedness from self-determination theory: opportunities to act, practise and receive support.

  1. Take an active role

    Ask for a hint when you need one. Some study groups choose a tutor; others receive an assignment.

    Autonomy: choices follow the study rules for your account.

  2. Practise step by step

    Work on finding percentages, percentage increases and percentage decreases, with feedback on your answers.

    Competence: practise with hints and answer feedback.

  3. Learn with guidance

    Explore different ways of presenting guidance while you practise. Your tutor is part of the learning application.

    Relatedness: a consistent tutor presence during your activities.
